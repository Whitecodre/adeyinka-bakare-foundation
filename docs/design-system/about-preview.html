The ABF journey

Support at every level

Each stage of your degree has a programme designed for it. Scroll to follow the path, or tap a stage.

100 Level: Need-Based Scholarship

Provides financial assistance to members who demonstrate genuine financial need, so they can focus on their studies.

  • Student of the Department of Information Technology
  • In 100 Level
  • A registered member of ABF
  • Meets any additional requirements approved by the Scholarship Committee

200 Level: Need-Based and Merit-Based

Need-based support continues, and the Merit-Based Scholarship recognises and rewards outstanding academic performance.

  • Student of the Department of Information Technology
  • In 200 Level
  • A registered member of ABF
  • Meets the academic performance requirements established by the Fellowship (merit)

300 Level: Internship Programme

Practical industry exposure and professional experience, so members build workplace competencies before graduation.

  • Student of the Department of Information Technology
  • In 300 Level
  • A registered member of ABF
  • Meets requirements set by the Fellowship or its partner organisations

400 Level: Mentorship Programme

Prepares final-year students for life after graduation by connecting them with experienced professionals and mentors.

  • Career guidance and professional development
  • Leadership coaching
  • CV and interview preparation
  • Networking opportunities and industry mentorship

Governance and transparency

Run by a written constitution

Funds come from sponsors, alumni, partners and well-wishers. They are used solely for ABF's objectives, and every expenditure is approved before it is paid. Records of income and spending are kept and reported to the Executive Council and members.
Discipline follows a written code: a written complaint, written notice and the chance to respond, an investigation by a committee, and a final decision by the Executive Council. Anyone disciplined may appeal in writing within seven days.
ABF is governed by a written constitution that sets out its vision, membership, leadership, programmes and finances.
