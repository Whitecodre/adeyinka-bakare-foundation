CHAPTER ONE 
PREAMBLE 
We, the members of the Adeyinka Bakare Fellowship Student Association, united by a commitment to academic 
excellence, leadership, integrity, innovation, and service, hereby adopt this constitution as the guiding framework 
for the governance and administration of the fellowship.  
The Adeyinka Bakare Fellowship exists to empower students through scholarships, mentorship, career 
development, and collaborative learning while fostering an environment of excellence, accountability, innovation 
and mutual support. 
ARTICLE 1: NAME 
The name of the organization shall be ADEYINKA BAKARE FELLOWSHIP, which can be referred to as ABF 
FELLOWSHIP. 
ARTICLE 2: VISION, MISSION AND OBJECTIVES 
Section 1: VISION: 
To support intentional undergraduate students of the Department of Information Technology with a strong 
commitment to learning and good character through its fellowship programme, enabling them to achieve academic 
success, complete their studies at the University and prepare them for their career pursuits. 
Section 2: MISSION: 
ABF Fellowship shall: 
● Provide need-based and merit-based scholarship opportunities and grants. 
● Promote mentorship and career development 
● Build a supportive academic and professional community. 
● Encourage leadership, innovation, and lifelong learning. 
● Connect members with opportunities for growth and impact. 
Section 3: OBJECTIVES 
The objectives of the ABF Fellowship are to: 
● Support deserving students of the Department of Information Technology through scholarship initiatives. 
● Establish a mentorship programme. 
● Promote networking among students and professionals. 
● Develop leadership skills among members. 
● Encourage innovation and collaboration. 
● Build a career-focused clusters for specialized learning. 
ARTICLE 3: MEMBERSHIP & MEETINGS 
Section 3.1: MEETINGS 
The fellowship shall hold 
● General Meetings 
● Executive Meetings 
● Emergency Meetings when necessary 
Notice of the meetings shall be communicated in advance. 
Section 3.2: Membership Eligibility 
Membership shall be open to students who satisfy the following requirements: 
1. Must be a registered student of the University of Ilorin. 
2. Must be a registered student of  any levels of the Department of Information Technology. 
3. Must register with the fellowship through the Fellowship registration link. 
Section 3.3: Categories of Membership 
● Active Members 
● Executive Members 
● Alumni Members 
Section 3.4: Rights Of Members 
Every member shall have the right to: 
● Be treated fairly and respectfully. 
● Vote for and contest any executive position. 
● Participate in Fellowship programmes 
● Access fellowship opportunities. 
● Receive mentorship support. 
Section 3.5: RESPONSIBILITIES OF MEMBERS 
● Members must uphold the values of the fellowship. 
● Members are mandated to attend meetings and programmes of the fellowship. 
● Members must respect Fellowship executives and their decisions 
● Members must promote unity and professionalism. 
● Members must protect the reputation of the Fellowship. 
● Members must abide by this constitution. 
CHAPTER TWO 
ARTICLE 4: EXECUTIVE OFFICERS 
The Fellowship shall be managed by elected or appointed executives, including: 
● President 
● General Secretary 
● Public Relation Officer 
● Financial Secretary 
● Cluster Coordinator 
● Graphic Designer/ Design Officer 
Section 4.1: DUTIES OF EXECUTIVE OFFICERS 
Each executive shall perform the responsibilities assigned to their office as approved by the Fellowship. The roles 
and responsibilities of the respective executive position are as follows: 
1. President 
The President is the association's chief leader and ensures that the vision and goals are met. 
Responsibilities: 
● Provide overall leadership and strategic direction for the Association. 
● Represent the Association at formal functions and external engagements. 
● Call and preside over all executive and general meetings. 
● Oversee all Association activities and ensure goals are achieved. 
● Appoint committees and delegates as needed. 
● Ensure the executive council fulfils its responsibilities. 
2. Public Relations Officer (PRO) 
The PRO manages the Association’s communication and public image. 
Responsibilities: 
● Develop and implement communication strategies for the Association. 
● Promote events, achievements, and announcements through social media and other channels. 
● Act as a liaison between the Association and external stakeholders. 
● Handle inquiries and press releases. 
● Build and maintain positive relationships with the public. 
3. General Secretary 
The General Secretary manages administrative tasks and documentation. 
Responsibilities: 
● Prepare meeting agendas in consultation with the President. 
● Record and keep accurate minutes of all meetings. 
● Handle official correspondence and documentation. 
● Maintain membership records and executive decisions. 
● Notify members of meetings and important announcements. 
● Ensure official records are up-to-date and safely kept. 
4. Designer 
The Designer is responsible for creating and maintaining the visual identity of the Association. 
Responsibilities: 
● Design flyers, posters, banners, certificates, and promotional materials. 
● Create graphics for social media and digital channels. 
● Ensure all designs reflect the Association’s brand identity. 
● Work closely with the PRO and media team to promote events. 
● Deliver designs promptly for all scheduled programs. 
5. Cluster Coordinator 
The Cluster Coordinator oversees all career clusters within the Association. 
Responsibilities: 
● Coordinate and supervise activities of all career clusters. 
● Work with Cluster Leads to develop cluster plans and initiatives. 
● Monitor participation and engagement in clusters. 
● Organize inter-cluster collaborations and events. 
● Report cluster activities and progress to the executive council. 
● Ensure all clusters align with the Association’s mission. 
6. Financial Secretary 
The Financial Secretary manages the Association’s financial records. 
Responsibilities: 
● Record all income, expenses, and financial transactions. 
● Maintain accurate financial books and reports. 
● Prepare regular financial updates for the executive council and members. 
● Collaborate with the President and Treasurer (if applicable) on budgeting. 
● Ensure financial transparency and accountability. 
● Keep all financial documents safely archived.

CHAPTER THREE 
ARTICLE 6: FINANCE 
Section 6.1: Sources of Funds 
The Fellowship shall receive financial support primarily through sponsorships, alumni, partners and other 
well-wishers who share the vision and objectives of the Fellowship 
Section 6.2: Management of Funds 
The Financial Secretary shall serve as the custodian of the Fellowship’s finances and shall be responsible for the 
proper management, documentation, and accountability of all funds received and disbursed on behalf of the 
Fellowship. 
The Financial Secretary shall maintain accurate financial records and present financial reports to the Executive 
Council and members as required. 
Section 6.3: Utilization of Funds  
Funds received by the Fellowship shall be used solely for the achievement of the Fellowship’s objectives, including 
but not limited to: 
● Awarding scholarships to eligible members. 
● Supporting internship and mentorship programmes. 
● Organizing Fellowship activities, events, workshops, and training programmes. 
● Meeting approved administrative and operational expenses of the Fellowship.  
Section 6.4: Authorization of Expenditures 
The President and the Executive Council shall approve all expenditures in accordance with the Fellowship’s financial 
policies before any funds are disbursed. The Financial Secretary shall only disburse funds that the appropriate 
authority has duly approved. 
Section 6.5: Accountability and Transparency 
All financial transactions of the Fellowship shall be conducted with integrity, transparency, and accountability. 
Proper records of all income and expenditure shall be maintained, and financial reports shall be made available to 
the Executive Council and members at appropriate intervals. 
ARTICLE 7: DISCIPLINARY MEASURES 
Section 7.1: Objectives and Scope 
The purpose of this Disciplinary Code is to promote discipline, accountability, respect, integrity, and unity within 
the Adeyinka Bakare Fellowship (ABF). 
This Code applies to all registered members and Executive Council members of the Fellowship. 
This disciplinary process shall be guided by fairness, impartiality, and the right of every member to be heard before 
any disciplinary action is taken. 
Section 7.2: Classification of Misconducts 
A. Minor Misconduct 
● Two consecutive absences from compulsory meetings or programmes without a valid excuse. 
● Repeated lateness to official meetings or Fellowship programmes. 
● Disruptive behaviour during meetings or Fellowship programmes. 
● Failure to complete assigned tasks without reasonable explanation. 
● Disrespectful conduct towards members or executives. 
Sanctions: 
● Verbal counselling 
● Written warning 
● Temporary removal from assigned responsibility. 
● Community service or any corrective assignment approved by the Executive Council. 
B. Major Misconduct  
● Repeated minor offences after previous warnings. 
● Open insubordination or refusal to comply with lawful decisions of the Fellowship. 
● Misrepresentation of the Fellowship or unauthorized use of its name or logo. 
● Posting false or damaging information about the Fellowship on social media. 
● Deliberately causing division or conflict among members. 
● Negligence by an executive that significantly affects Fellowship activities. 
Sanctions: 
● Final written warning. 
● Suspension from committees or leadership responsibilities. 
● Suspension of membership privileges for a specified period. 
● Removal from a leadership position (for executives only). 
C. Gross Misconduct 
● Misappropriation or theft of Fellowship funds or property. 
● Fraud, forgery, or financial misconduct. 
● Physical violence, bullying, harassment, or intimidation. 
● Sexual harassment or abuse. 
● Deliberate damage to Fellowship property 
● Any criminal act that brings the Fellowship into serious disrepute. 
Sanctions: 
● Immediate suspension pending investigation. 
● Removal from office (where applicable). 
● Expulsion from the Fellowship. 
● Restitution where necessary. 
● Referral to the appropriate university authorities where applicable. 
Section 7.3: Additional Responsibilities of Executives 
Executives shall be held to a higher standard of conduct because they serve as leaders and representatives of the 
Fellowship. 
An executive may be disciplined for: 
● Abuse of office or authority. 
● Failure to account for Fellowship funds or property under their care. 
● Failure to carry out assigned constitutional responsibilities without reasonable cause. 
● Persistent absenteeism from Executive Council meetings. 
● Conduct that brings the Fellowship into disrepute. 
● Breach of confidentiality. 
Sanctions: 
● Written warning will be given to such executive. 
● Suspension from office. 
● Removal from office. 
● Disqualification from holding executive office for a specified period, where appropriate. 
Section 7.4: Disciplinary Committee 
The Executive Council shall constitute an ad hoc Disciplinary Committee whenever necessary. 
The Committee shall consist of: 
● The President (Chairperson), except where there is a conflict of interest. 
● Two impartial members appointed by the Executive Council. 
● One representative of the Board of Trustees. 
Any member directly involved in the matter shall not serve on the committee. 
Section 7.5: Disciplinary Procedures 
1. A written complaint shall be submitted to the General Secretary or President. 
2. The accused member shall receive written notice of the allegation and shall be allowed to respond. 
3. The Disciplinary Committee shall investigate the matter and hear all relevant parties. 
4. The Committee shall submit its recommendations to the Executive Council. 
5. The Executive Council shall make the final decision, except where the Constitution provides otherwise. 
Any member disciplined under this Code may appeal in writing within seven (7) days of receiving the decision. 
ARTICLE 8: ADOPTION OF CONSTITUTION 
Section 8.1: Adoption of the Constitution 
This Constitution shall be deemed adopted upon its approval by the founding members or the duly constituted 
governing body of the Adeyinka Bakare Fellowship. 
Section 8.2: Supremacy of the Constitution 
Upon adoption, this Constitution shall become the supreme governing document of the Adeyinka Bakare 
Fellowship. All officers, committees, and members shall conduct the affairs of the Fellowship in accordance with its 
provisions. 
Section 8.3: Compliance 
Every registered member and officer of the Fellowship shall be deemed to have accepted and agreed to abide by 
the provisions of this Constitution upon becoming a member of the Fellowship. 
Section 8.4: Commencement of Adoption 
This Constitution shall come into force on the date of its adoption and shall remain in effect until duly amended in 
accordance with the provisions of this Constitution. 
ARTICLE 9: AMENDMENT OF CONSTITUTION 
Section 9.1: Right to Amend 
This Constitution may be amended whenever it is considered necessary to improve the governance, effectiveness, 
or operations of the Adeyinka Bakare Fellowship, provided that such amendment remains consistent with the 
vision, mission, and objectives of the Fellowship. 
Section 9.2: Proposal of Amendments 
A proposal to amend this Constitution may be initiated by: 
● The Executive Council; or 
● Any registered member of the Fellowship, provided the proposal is supported by at least one-third (1/3) of 
the registered members. 
Section 9.3: Notice of Proposed Amendments 
All proposed amendments shall be submitted in writing to the General Secretary and communicated to members at 
least fourteen (14) days before the meeting at which they will be considered. 
Section 9.4: Approval of Amendments 
An amendment shall be adopted only upon receiving the approval of not less than two-third (2/3) of the eligible 
members present and voting at a duly constituted meeting of the Fellowship. 
Section 9.5: Effective Date 
An approved amendment shall take effect immediately upon its adoption unless otherwise stated in the resolution 
approving the amendment.
